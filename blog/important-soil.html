How much plant-available water is in the Hyalite root zone?

Written by Grace Koolman · October 16, 2026

The project calculation reports 3.2 inches of plant-available water (PAW) associated with 31.1 inches of soil. Dividing the reported PAW by the reported soil thickness gives approximately 10.3% (3.2 ÷ 31.1), not 83%.

The original project note describes the PAW estimate as potentially overestimated because the CDM/Welsh triangles estimate field capacity (FC) and permanent wilting point (PWP) from texture without accounting for two factors identified in the note: rock fragments and soil organic matter (SOM).

The note reasons that rock does not absorb much water compared with SOM, which acts like a sponge. If a very cobbly soil is treated as though it were fine-textured throughout, the estimated PAW may be too high.

Interpretation note: The supplied source contains both the figures 3.2 inches, 31.1 inches, and “83% PAW.” The arithmetic ratio of the first two values is about 10.3%, so the 83% figure should be checked against the original calculation before presentation.
